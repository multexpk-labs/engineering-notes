# Engineering Note Template

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What system, application, or experiment is involved?

## Problem / Question

What needed to be solved or understood?

## Evidence

Commands, logs, symptoms, metrics, or reproducible observations.

## Diagnosis

What the evidence indicates. Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses.

## Change

What was changed and why.

## Verification

How the result was tested.

## Rollback

How to safely undo the change if required.

## Lesson

What should be remembered or reused.

## Related Work

Link related repositories or notes.
