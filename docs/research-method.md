# Research and Reimplementation Method

MULTEXPK LABS uses a practical engineering research workflow:

**Find → Clone → Inspect → Understand → Document → Reimplement → Test → Improve**

## Research rules

- Prefer public source code and documentation.
- Identify the license before reusing code.
- Preserve required attribution and notices.
- Distinguish observation from inference.
- Reimplement proprietary behavior independently rather than copying protected source.
- Use synthetic data for public demonstrations.
- Keep credentials and private infrastructure out of research repositories.

The objective is understanding, reproducibility, and original engineering work.
