# Architecture Decision Record Template

## Title

Short decision name.

## Status

Proposed / Accepted / Superseded / Rejected

## Context

Describe the technical problem and constraints.

## Options

List realistic alternatives without assuming the preferred answer.

## Decision

Document the selected approach and its scope.

## Consequences

Record benefits, trade-offs, operational impact, and future constraints.

## Verification

Define how the decision will be validated.
